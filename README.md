# Little Frames

Your favourite photos, pinned to your Windows desktop as little Polaroids.

**Website:** [juhyeonie.github.io/Little-Frames](https://juhyeonie.github.io/Little-Frames/)

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/juhyeonie/Little-Frames/releases/latest)**:

- **Little Frames Setup x.y.z.exe**: the installer (recommended). Installs for your account only, no admin prompt.
- **Little Frames x.y.z.exe**: portable version, runs without installing.

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The app isn't code-signed yet, so the first time you run it Windows SmartScreen may say it
"protected your PC". Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's new in 1.0.1

- Frames can always be dragged and repositioned. Before, they sometimes ignored the mouse until you opened
  the app, especially after **Win+D / Show desktop**.
- After Show desktop, frames stay visible on the desktop instead of hiding behind it, and slip back behind
  your apps when your windows return.

Already on 1.0.0? Install 1.0.1 over it; your photos, frames and settings are kept.

## What it does

- Multiple Polaroid widgets that float on your desktop: drag them anywhere, resize from the corner
- Your own photos: drag & drop, pick individual photos, or choose a folder
- A new random photo every hour, or click a Polaroid to change it right away
- Paper colours, tilt, shadows, washi tape and stickers, a handwritten clock, and little messages
- Remembers every position, size and style, and can start with Windows

Your photos never leave your PC.
