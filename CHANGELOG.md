# Changelog

All notable changes to Little Frames. Downloads are on the [Releases](https://github.com/juhyeonie/Little-Frames/releases) page.

## 1.0.1 (2026-09-29)

### Fixed
- Frames can always be dragged and repositioned. Before, they sometimes ignored the mouse until you opened
  the app, especially after **Win+D / Show desktop**. Whether a frame takes the mouse is now decided from the
  real cursor position and the frame's exact shape, tilt and resize corner included.
- After Show desktop, frames stay visible on the desktop instead of hiding behind it, and slip back behind your
  apps when your windows return.

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-28)

First release.

- Polaroid widgets that float on your Windows desktop: drag, resize from the corner, as many as you like
- Your own photos: drag & drop, pick individual photos, or choose a folder that's watched for new images
- A new random photo every hour (or 15m / 30m / 3h / daily), or click a frame to change it, with an
  instant-film "developing" transition
- Right-click menu, paper colours, tilt, shadows, washi tape and stickers
- Handwritten clock and date, and little messages under the photo, beside it, or on a sticky note
- "On desktop" or "Always on top" layers
- Remembers every position, size and style, and can start with Windows
